PROGRESS AND EXTERNAL SERVICES
Learning notes and progress are stored in this browser; the application does not
upload them to a learning database. A shared browser profile can see local records.
Exports contain the learner's own records and should be shared deliberately.

The reel's YouTube preview thumbnails can load before a video is played. Video
players and the YouTube player API load only after the visitor clicks to load an
excerpt. The default embed uses youtube-nocookie.com. If it fails, a separately
labeled button lets the visitor explicitly choose the standard youtube.com player.
Either player connects to YouTube and may involve its cookies or other processing.
Direct publisher links are also available. The site uses Google Fonts and Vercel
hosting, which involve ordinary external requests.
