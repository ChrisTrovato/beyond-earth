Beyond Earth is free to explore and does not require an account.

What the site saves

Lesson progress, field notes, review history, project records, study sessions, settlement drafts, reading positions, and roadmap choices, arcade achievements, reel bookmarks, and design-studio experiments are stored in this browser using local storage. The application does not upload those records to a user profile or learning database.

Keeping a copy

Mission Control lets you export and import your learning log. That export includes lessons, lab visits, notes, study sessions, learning paths, reviews, and project records. Settlement drafts, book reading positions, roadmap checklists, arcade achievements, reel bookmarks, and design-studio experiments have separate local storage and are not included. The design studio has its own experiment-log export. Clearing site data can remove all local progress.

What is public

The lessons, artwork, diagrams, field guide, and manuscript are public educational resources. The public edition uses general learning examples. Notes you write in the app are not added to these published files. An exported log contains your own records, so choose where you keep or share it.

Hosting and external services

Vercel hosts the site and can process standard web-request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. The main site loads its font from Google Fonts. Links to outside learning resources take you to services with their own policies. The application does not include an advertising pixel or an analytics tracker.

Watching the motivation reel

The reel lists original talks and connects them to lessons. Preview thumbnails load from YouTube when their cards appear. Video players load only when you choose to watch. The default embedded player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain. If that player cannot load, you can choose the clearly labeled standard YouTube player. Either option connects to YouTube and may involve its cookies or other processing. The original video remains on its publisher’s channel. You can also open it directly on YouTube.

Shared devices

Someone using the same browser profile can see its saved progress. Pages on the same web origin share a browser security boundary. Avoid putting passwords, payment details, or other sensitive records into field notes.
